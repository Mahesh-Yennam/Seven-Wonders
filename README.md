# 🏛️ 7 Wonders of the World Explorer — Gamility

An interactive, high-fidelity encyclopedic voyage across humanity's greatest architectural monuments and nature's grandest spectacles.

## Features
- **3 Distinct Eras & Categories (21 Wonders)**:
  - **New 7 Wonders of the World**: Great Wall of China, Petra, Colosseum, Chichén Itzá, Machu Picchu, Taj Mahal, Christ the Redeemer.
  - **7 Wonders of the Ancient World**: Great Pyramid of Giza, Hanging Gardens of Babylon, Temple of Artemis, Statue of Zeus, Mausoleum at Halicarnassus, Colossus of Rhodes, Lighthouse of Alexandria.
  - **7 Natural Wonders of the World**: Grand Canyon, Great Barrier Reef, Harbor of Rio de Janeiro, Mount Everest, Aurora Borealis, Parícutin Volcano, Victoria Falls.
- **Uncropped Master Photography**: Full architectural compositions displayed in high resolution with `object-fit: contain` and zero dark gradient overlays.
- **Full Dossier Inspector**: Click any card or "Inspect Dossier" button to open detailed historical specifications, builder dynasty, GPS coordinates, materials, purpose, and engineering secrets.
- **Interactive World Map**: Visual globe projection plotting all 21 monuments with pin navigation.
- **Chronological Timeline**: Comparative 4,500-year historical slider from 2580 BC to modern era.
- **Wonder Duel & Quiz**: Test your archaeological and geographical knowledge.
- **Multi-Factor Search & Filters**: Instant search by name, country, material, or civilizational era.

## Architecture
- Standalone subfolder application conforming to the Gamility Standalone Apps specification.
- Zero build tools required; runs natively with vanilla HTML5, modern CSS variables, and modular ES6 JavaScript.
